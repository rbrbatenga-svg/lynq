Help & Setup Guide

LYNQ

How to set up your card

Tap your LYNQ card with your phone. When the page opens, click "Sign in" at the bottom to connect your Google account and claim your card.

Why won't the Sign in button work sometimes?

Sometimes apps like Facebook, Messenger, or Instagram trap web pages inside a tiny helper window that blocks Google from opening. The app gets confused and hides your editing powers, making you look like a regular visitor!

How to fix it and edit your card

• Open in a real browser: Tap the three dots (menu) in the corner of your app and choose "Open in Chrome" or "Open in Safari".

• Use Incognito / Private mode: Copy your card link, open a new Incognito tab in your browser, paste the link, and press Sign in safely.
